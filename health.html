Keeping Your Dog Healthy

A healthy dog needs proper nutrition, exercise, clean water and regular veterinary care. Looking after your dog's health helps it live a happier and more comfortable life.

Common Dog Health Problems

1. Skin Problems

Dogs may experience itching, irritation or hair loss. A veterinarian can help identify the cause.

2. Ear Infections

Ear infections may cause scratching, redness or unusual discharge.

3. Dental Problems

Poor dental care can lead to bad breath, gum problems and discomfort.

Signs Your Dog May Be Unwell

  • Loss of appetite
  • Unusual tiredness
  • Repeated vomiting or diarrhoea
  • Changes in behaviour
  • Difficulty breathing

Contact a veterinarian if your dog seems unwell. Difficulty breathing or sudden severe symptoms require urgent veterinary attention.

Vaccinations and Check-ups

Vaccinations help protect dogs against certain serious diseases. Regular veterinary check-ups help identify health problems early.

Ask your veterinarian which vaccinations and check-up schedule are appropriate for your dog.

Tips for a Healthy Dog

  • Provide clean drinking water every day.
  • Feed balanced and suitable dog food.
  • Give your dog regular exercise.
  • Brush your dog's coat when needed.
  • Keep up with veterinary check-ups.
  • Follow professional advice for flea and tick prevention.
